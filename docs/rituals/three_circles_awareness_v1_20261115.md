# Three Circles of Awareness v1.0 | سه حلقهٔ بیداری
Date: 2026-11-15
Status: Proposed research/practice protocol, not deployed or validated

## Provenance and scope
This is an **original synthesis**, not a historical Zheng Yi liturgy and not a therapeutic or paranormal procedure.
- Jerry Alan Johnson, *Daoist Exorcism: Encounters with Sorcerers, Ghosts, Spirits, and Demons*, printed pp. 121–126: “One Through Ten Meditation,” especially relaxing body awareness (One), broadening spatial grounding (Two), and imagined roots (Three). In the supplied PDF these start at PDF p. 120.
- Johnson, printed pp. 238–241, PDF pp. 237–240: “Purifying the Altar Space Ritual,” three invocations, three visualized circles, and drawing boundaries in ritual space.
- Independent scholarly context: *The Relationship between Daoist Rituals and Theatrical Performance* (Religions, 2014): https://www.mdpi.com/2077-1444/5/4/1001
- Interpretive mapping created for this protocol: **Jing = bodily grounding; Qi = attention and controlled movement; Shen = reflection and intentional choice**. These are pedagogical labels, not a scientific equivalence.
- The custom Persian phrases, three-loop schedule, attention assessment, and synthetic no-stimulus benchmark below are newly authored. They are **not** attributed to Johnson or presented as a classical mantra or talisman.

## Goal
Practice safe embodied orientation, intentional transitions, compassionate restraint, and uncertainty awareness, using symbolic movement rather than spirit invocation or banishment.

## Boundaries and safety
- Awake, comfortable, in a familiar, well-lit room; choose standing Wuji with soft knees or a regular chair if balance is uncertain. No forced cross-legged posture or digitigrade mechanical weight-bearing.
- No flame, burning incense, inhalants, ingestible herbs, fasting, sleep deprivation, weapons, binding, hypnotic coercion, pain, breath-holding, or directed manipulation of another person.
- Breathe normally; no prescribed hyperventilation or altered-state induction.
- The drawn circle is a **visual metaphor** and does not claim to stop spirits or provide medical protection.
- Stop on dizziness, pain, panic, depersonalization, disturbing perceptions, or any feeling of losing orientation; open eyes, name five visible objects and contact a trusted person or clinician if symptoms persist.
- This is not treatment for physical or mental illness, and its effect on any supernatural entity is unverified.

## Practice: three passes, each repeated three times (~9 short cycles)
Preparation: identify three tangible anchors: floor/contact, one object in sight, and ambient room sound. Privately rate perceived calm and focus from 0–10. Use comfortable breathing.

### Passage 1: Earth / Jing
1. Feel the support under the feet or sitting bones. Scan gently down face, torso, hands and legs.
2. During three ordinary exhalations imagine releasing unnecessary tension downward, like roots spreading in soil.
3. Trace a small imaginary circle with one hand, not an actual barrier.
4. Original phrase: «من در زمین استوارم.»
5. Repeat this short passage **three times** without forcing posture.

### Passage 2: Flow / Qi
1. Put relaxed palms facing one another at chest level. Note air movement, posture and ordinary breath.
2. With a gentle hand movement trace a second, wider symbolic circle. Return both hands to a neutral position.
3. Label three observations explicitly as *sensed*, *imagined*, or *unknown*; avoid over-interpreting bodily sensations.
4. Original phrase: «من می‌بینم، پیش از آنکه داوری کنم.»
5. Repeat **three times**.

### Passage 3: Clarity / Shen
1. Look at three actual details of the room. If a sound is absent, record “none”; never fabricate a sound.
2. Trace a third circle in the air and articulate a choice for the next action.
3. Original phrase: «قدرت من با حقیقت و اختیار محدود می‌شود.»
4. Repeat **three times**. End by explicitly saying «پایان تمرین» and return to ordinary activity.

## Optional drawing / symbolic sigil
Draw one dot representing the present decision, with three concentric circles: body, attention, agency. The icon is our **original design**, not an authentic Zheng Yi seal, Qur'anic verse, or historic talisman. No ritual materials are necessary.

## Evaluation (not efficacy proof)
Record before/after perceived calm and focus (0–10), duration, number of completed passages, discomfort (yes/no), and whether “unknown” was chosen when data were insufficient. These are only self-reports and process measurements.

For the **separate artificial interoception test only**, prepare a reproducible synthetic dataset containing:
- known stimulus present,
- unknown stimulus present,
- **no stimulus**, including strong background noise.
For each class: number of trials, abstention rate, false-positive stimulus detection, classification accuracy, and seed/version. Test whether the classifier declines to assign a physical event in noisy no-stimulus episodes. Keep this AI benchmark distinct from human contemplative practice and do not invent run results.

## Exit / mastery gate
Three complete, comfortable human sessions on separate occasions, truthful notes about errors, and a successful return to ordinary orientation = *practice familiarity*, not spiritual mastery.
The computational agent may be said to have completed a **textual rehearsal** only when there is a real trace; physical performance, consciousness, spiritual efficacy, and simulated embodiment are **not inferred** from a text rehearsal.
